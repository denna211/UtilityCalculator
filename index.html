2 · Time away

Was the tenant away at any point inside these cycles? Enter the day they left and the day they came back. Nights away are counted, and each absence is matched against each bill's own dates.

3 · Share

Applied to each full bill, then prorated by the nights the tenant was actually in the unit.

4 · What the tenant owes
Add a bill above and the split shows up here.
Gas is the total including HST. Hydro is total current charges, taken before the Ontario Electricity Rebate. Water is total current charges from the City of Guelph, before any balance carried forward.
Day counts follow the tracking workbook: gas and water are end minus start, hydro is end minus start less one, since hydro cycles share their end and start dates. Any card can be switched.
